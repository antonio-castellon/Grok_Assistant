# What you can say

Start with `hola grok` or `¿estás ahí?`. When no conversation is active, other commands begin with `comando`. Phrases longer than six words are normally ignored unless they are a valid greeting or `pon la canción` followed by a title of up to sixteen words. Once you are in a conversation, that six-word limit no longer applies. Music requests do not need `comando`, and goodbye is handled locally and immediately. While a cloud answer is on its way, the assistant plays a short waiting phrase, pauses the microphone, and then speaks the reply. The information window contains the full command list.

The assistant lives in the system tray. Left-click the icon to open its window and right-click it to open the menu. Closing the window only hides it; use **Salir** in the window or tray menu to quit completely.
