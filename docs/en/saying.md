# What you can say

Start with `hola grok` or `¿estás ahí?`. Without the name, `comando pregunta`, `comando abrir charla`, or `comando abrir conversación` opens the chat. It closes on thanks or okay, or when the silence reaches the minutes on the Simple tab. When no conversation is active, other commands begin with `comando`. Phrases longer than six words are normally ignored unless they are a valid greeting or `pon la canción` followed by a title of up to sixteen words. Once you are in a conversation, that six-word limit no longer applies. Music requests do not need `comando`, and goodbye is handled locally and immediately. While a cloud answer is on its way, the assistant plays a short waiting phrase, pauses the microphone, and then speaks the reply. The information window contains the full command list.

On the Simple tab, How the chat starts is text or phoneme. Text is the current behaviour: the listening engine closes the phrase and the name is looked up in those words. Phoneme, while the chat is closed, listens only for the sound of a prepared phrase and does not transcribe the room. Whatever follows in that same audio goes to the listening engine already chosen. The keyboard, a name other than grok, or a missing model or phoneme line stays on text.

The assistant lives in the system tray. Left-click the icon to open its window and right-click it to open the menu. Closing the window only hides it; use **Salir** in the window or tray menu to quit completely.
